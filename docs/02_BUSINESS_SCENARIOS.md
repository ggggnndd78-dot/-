# 02 Business Scenarios

## Customer scenario

1. Customer enters phone or email.
2. Backend generates OTP.
3. OTP is sent by SMS or email.
4. Customer verifies OTP.
5. Customer account becomes active.
6. App opens customer interface.

## Merchant scenario

1. Merchant owner registers.
2. Merchant verifies OTP.
3. Merchant submits store information, branch, bank account, working hours, and documents.
4. Organization status becomes pending review.
5. Admin approves or rejects.
6. If approved, merchant dashboard and employees/branches become available.

## Workshop scenario

Same as merchant, with workshop services, workshop branch data, and service operations.

## Warehouse scenario

Same as merchant, with warehouse profile and wholesale inventory preparation.

## Employee scenario

Employee is created by organization owner, receives access, and can only use granted permissions and branches.
