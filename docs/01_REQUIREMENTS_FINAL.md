# 01 Requirements Final

## Product principle

Ghiyarak is a multi-role automotive enterprise platform for spare parts, workshops, warehouses, employees, admin review, and future commerce operations.

## Primary flows in this package

- Guest can browse but cannot buy or book.
- Customer can register with OTP and become active directly.
- Merchant can register, complete business onboarding, upload documents, and wait for approval.
- Workshop can register, complete workshop onboarding, upload documents, and wait for approval.
- Warehouse can register, complete warehouse onboarding, upload documents, and wait for approval.
- Admin can review verification requests.
- Organization owner can manage branches and employees according to approval and permissions.

## SMS provider

The working SMS provider is TextPeak Streams API event_send.
